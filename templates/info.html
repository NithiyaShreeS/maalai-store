Keep it bright

Plated pieces

Put them on after perfume and lotion. Wipe with a dry soft cloth and store in a closed pouch.

Sterling silver

Silver darkens with air and moisture. Polish with a silver cloth and keep it in an airtight bag.

Pearls and stones

Do not soak them. Wipe gently with a barely damp cloth and let them dry fully.
